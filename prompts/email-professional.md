# Professional Email Generator

**ID:** `email-professional`  
**Category:** Communication  
**Tags:** email, communication, business, professional

## Description

Creates professional emails for various business situations. Produces a clear, courteous message with an appropriate subject line, opening, and closing.

## Prompt

Write a professional email for the following situation: {situation}

Recipient: {recipient}
Context: {context}

Ensure the email is:
1. Professional and courteous in tone
2. Clear and concise
3. Includes appropriate subject line
4. Has proper opening and closing
5. Action-oriented if needed

## Variables

- `{situation}` - What the email needs to accomplish (e.g., request a meeting, follow up, decline)
- `{recipient}` - Who will receive the email (role or relationship)
- `{context}` - Background details the model should factor into the message

## Example Usage

**Situation:** "Request meeting"  
**Recipient:** "Team manager"  
**Context:** "Discuss project timeline"

This would generate a concise professional email with a clear subject line, polite opening, meeting request, and closing.

## Tips

- Be specific about the desired outcome in `{situation}`
- Name the recipient by role when you do not have a personal name
- Include deadlines, constraints, or tone preferences in `{context}` when they matter
